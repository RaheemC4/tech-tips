# Intel Overclocking Guidelines

*Category: Overclocking | Originally posted: 2024-06-01 | 4 message(s)*

Here are some useful guidelines to follow when overclocking the following Intel CPUs

---
**Raheem** (2024-06-01):

**10th Gen (CometLake)**

10700K & 10900K - Disable Hyperthreading

10850K - Disable Hyperthreading

10600K - Leave hyperthreading enabled

Ring - For 99% of people, do not go above 4.8GHz, as it is usually massively unstable and will destroy performance if you try pushing higher. You can always test for yourself measuring 1% and 0.1% lows to see what performs the best. 10900Ks and 10700Ks can usually do 4.8GHz no problem, 10600Ks usually around 4.6GHz.

**11th Gen (RocketLake)**

11600K - Enable Hyperthreading

11700K & 11900K - Disable Hyperthreading

Ring - 4.5-4.8GHz

---
**Raheem** (2024-06-01):

**12th Gen: (Alderlake)
**
12600K: E-Cores Enabled | Hyperthreading Disabled

12700K: E-Cores Disabled | Hyperthreading Enabled

12900K/KS: E-Cores Enabled | Hyperthreading Disabled

Ring: 
E-Cores Enabled: 4.2-4.4GHz 
E-Cores Disabled: 4.6-4.8GHz

---
**Raheem** (2024-06-01):

**13th/14th Gen: (Raptorlake)**

13600K: E-Cores Enabled | Hyperthreading (Test in games you play)

13700K: E-Cores Enabled | Hyperthreading Disabled

13900K/KS: E-Cores Enabled | Hyperthreading Disabled

Ring: 4.7-4.9GHz
