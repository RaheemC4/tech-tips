# Framechasers Overclocking Course

*Category: Overclocking | Originally posted: 2024-09-24 | 55 message(s)*

Framechasers is a youtuber who has contributed a significant portion of my knowledge and understanding of ram/cpu overclocking and its benefits towards gaming. 

He has released a $400 course that he has given his OG discord members access to for free. I have downloaded the contents of the course and will upload them here for you guys to help somewhat bridge a gap between the spread misinformation of the public to the information in this course. This course may be the most useful and important resource that I can provide for you guys. Enjoy :Humble_Raheem:

Timings Cheat Sheet Arrow Lake

There are only two differences to be made on arrow lake CPU's

1) Secondary Timing TRTP = 16 (was 12 on Raptorlake)

2) Tertiary Timing tWRWR_SG = 24 (was 16 on Raptorlake)

if you use Raptorlake timings on those two the system will not POST

ALL OTHER TIMINGS AND VOLTAGES ARE THE SAME

Step By Step Tuning Cheat Sheet

1) Lock Memory Controller 1.45v
2) Lock Gear 2
3) Make It Just Work Module
4) 1 Hour OCCT Between All Changes

TUNE MEMORY FIRST
1) DDR5 Tuning Module (dont forget Arrow Lake Timings Cheat Sheet)
2) A die 8133 = Mdie 8800 Roughly

TUNE D2D SECOND
1) D2D Frequency
2) Stock is 0.78v, Max is 1.1v
3) Will Hit Wall Somewhere Between 34-40

TUNE NGU THIRD
1) NGU Frequency
2) Controlled By System Agent Voltage, Max 1.4v (Check for droop)
3) Will Hit Wall Somewhere Between 33-36

TUNE RING FOURTH
1) Ring Frequency
2) Controlled By Ring Voltage, Max 1.35v
3) Will Hit Wall Somewhere Between 40-44
