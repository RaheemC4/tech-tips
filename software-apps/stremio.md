**Raheem** (2025-06-25):

Stremio by default uses torrent streaming to playback Movies/TV Shows to you. The problem with streaming is that it requires you to download the torrent from its source while you are trying to watch it which causes long loading times to start watching, buffering and a long wait when trying to skip parts of the content. This will be in most cases because you have to rely on the torrent having lots of seeds for it to be faster.

---
**Raheem** (2025-06-25):

To fix this we use a **Debrid** service. This will integrate into stremio and provide netflix, disney+ etc levels of speed from the content because instead of downloading and streaming the media and hoping that it has enough seeds. A debrid is farm of servers that is widely used by people and contains a database of Movies/TV Shows that have been downloaded/cached to the servers which make it much faster to load and skip through.

DEBRID Setup


The debrid service we will use is the most popular one for stremio that has the mosts cached/downloaded content and is considered the fastest. It is called **Real-Debrid**. It costs £13 for 6 months and you will need to remember to manually renew it. The downside to this debrid is that it is limited to being used by 1 public IP at a time. That means that it can be used by an unlimited amount of people in one house hold but if someone with that has stremio setup with the debrid account leaves that house to use it, no one in the house can use it or the person who left cant use it while people at home use it

How to use


When you pick a movie or tv show episode you would like to watch, you will see that it will sort the torrents with **[RD+] **and then by **Torrentio**. This is normal and intended as we installed the torrentio real-debrid addon before the normal torrentio addon. Every time you go to watch something, **ALWAYS PICK RD+** if it is available. These will be the cached/downloaded ones that load very fast. All you have to do is pick the resolution you want to watch in and thats it! If Torrentio is down, you can still use the AIOStreams addon by selecting **AIOStreams | ElfHosted**

You now have the ultimate stremio setup and will no longer need to pay for another streaming service again.
