# Torrenting Games

*Category: Software & Apps | Originally posted: 2024-06-23 | 5 message(s)*

There are 2 main ways that people can download and play cracked/repacked games.

1. Direct Downloads

2. Torrenting


**Direct downloads** are very simple and perfect for smaller games that are under 5GB. Direct downlaods have a limitation in that larger sized games will be split up into multiple compressed parts. This means you will have to download more than one file and then later extract them into one folder in order to access the cracked/repacked game. The advantage is that direct downloads are faster than torrenting for smaller files.

**Torrenting** is the act of downloading and uploading files through the BitTorrent network. Instead of downloading files to a central server, torrenting involves downloading files from other users’ devices on the network. Torrents can help you download large files faster than traditional downloading methods, even if you have a slow internet connection.

VPN


A VPN is very important when torrenting pirated games as your internet service provider (virgin,sky,bt,talktalk,ee etc) can see your torrenting traffic and will know if you are torrenting something that is copyrighted/pirated. It is very common for people who are torrenting these pirated files to receive Copyright Cease and Desist letters in the post. Sometimes this will lead to police action or cancellation/barring of internet services from that provider. So using a VPN is a must.

**The VPN MUST have peer to peer server option** or it will not let u torrent. You will get an error and the download will stay stalled. So use a good VPN like mullvad/Nord/surfshark/express. They aren't just used for torrenting, they are also good for staying secure online so investing in a VPN is 100% worth it. I personally use NordVPN myself and my phone is 24/7 connected to it.
