In GTA 5 online, there is a way to get into a public session and have a solo lobby so that when you want to do CEO missions or heist preps you wont be disturbed by other players.
 



Open the game into a normal public session, then open resource monitor, right click GTA5.exe and click suspend process, wait 7 seconds, right click GTA5.exe and click resume process, close resource monitor and head back into the game, you should see that everyone else in the lobby should have left :D
